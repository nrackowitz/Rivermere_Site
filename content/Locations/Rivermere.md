#Location

- **Where it is:** Small town central to the story
- **First visited or heard about:** Start of campaign 

## Notes
#### Rivermere Description
- Relatively young settlement, only about three generations old
- Fewer than 400 residents
	- Small, tight-knit community
- Several days from [[Ashport]] and [[Harthaven]]
	- Largely out of the way of the wider world
- Founded by [[Acorn Mills]]
- Filled with half-buried ancient ruins left behind by the last eruption of [[Nicuashy]] 
	- Became home to all manner of monsters and dangerous creatures
	- Cleared by [[Acorn Mills]] and [[Hazel Mills]] over years
- Fought a Four-Year war against the werebears with assistance from magicians from [[Ashport]] 
- Currently led by [[Chief Thlayli]]
#### Life in Rivermere
- Maintains a relatively simple way of life
- Close relationships and connections with neighbors
- Regarded from the outside as little more than a gathering of hardy settlers, known little beyond the accomplishments of [[Chief Thlayli]]
