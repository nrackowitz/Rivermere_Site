## Notes
- Population 4,000-6,000
- Relatively modest town by regional standards
- Importance to the tobacco industry far exceeds its size
	- Industrial center for the tobacco industry
	- Contains tobacco workshops and manufacturing centers where harvested leaves are cured, sorted, blended, and prepared for sale
	- Specialized arcane practitioners also work throughout the industry, using their knowledge to infuse tobacco with magical properties
- Farmers prefer to keep to themselves, operating plantations independently and selling their harvests through established merchants
