## What we know
- An unusually thick, heavy fog that can descend upon the land with little warning
- Unlike ordinary fog, the Grey seems to swallow its surroundings, making it difficult to see, hear, or navigate even familiar places
	- Sounds become distorted; distances seem misleading, and there is an overwhelming feeling of unease
- Associated with disappearances, unexplained deaths, fires, thefts, strange screams, and other disturbing occurrences
	- Some claim to have seen shapes moving, heard voices calling their name, or witnessed other strange occurrences
- Not understood what it is or where it comes from
- Some warlocks claim that it is a source of their power
