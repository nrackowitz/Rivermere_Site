## What we know
- No universally accepted pantheon of divine beings
- No temples or organized religions 
- What people call a god is usually an extraordinarily powerful being, usually mortal in origin
	- Possess immense magical abilities, powerful artifacts, and knowledge far beyond that of ordinary spellcasters
	- Not necessarily immortal, all-knowing, or morally superior
