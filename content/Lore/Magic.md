## What we know
### Wizards
- Cannot simply decide to cast spells through natural talent or willpower
	- Learned discipline that requires access to a true Casting Tome
- Casting Tome
	- Quality is important
	- Some only contain certain spells or cover limited levels
	- Exceptionally rare tomes contain complete spells
	- Most wizards find upgraded tomes as they adventure
	- A wizard without a tome takes twice as long to level up
- Common perceptions
	- Admired by scholars and academics 
	- Merchants value their knowledge
	- Rural communities are either curious or suspicious
	- Employed as tutors by wealthy families
	- High level wizards are very respected
### Sorcerers 
- Described as people whose bodies are made to carry magic
- Often have quirks such as twitching fingers, restless movements, or occasional bursts of magical energy that escape without warning
- Have difficulty with written language, particularly complex symbols and magical notation
	- Struggle with reading or writing
	- Could be connected to the way they percieve magic
### Warlocks
- Generally regarded with suspiscion 
	- Often outright disliked
	- Obtained abilities through bargains, pacts, and relationships with powerful outside forces
- Source of power is of great concern
	- No universally accepted pantheon of [[Gods]], people are uncomfortable with those who claim to have contacted dieties directly
	- Many warlocks claim their powers come from [[The Grey]]
- Some think they are foolish to bargain with things they cannot understand
